**mathe** started with a simple belief: mental math isn't a gift you're born with — it's a skill you can train, and it's a lot more fun when there's someone to race.

## Our mission

We want to make mental math a game worth playing every day. Not endless worksheets — real shortcuts the pros use, learned in minutes, then put to the test in fast, fair duels against real people.

## Why duels

Practicing alone is easy to skip. A live opponent changes everything: suddenly speed matters, focus sharpens, and a two-minute match turns into "just one more". Rating-based matchmaking keeps every duel close, so wins feel earned and losses feel winnable.

## What makes mathe different

- **Tricks, not tricks-of-memory.** Every topic teaches a genuine method you can reuse for life.
- **Fair by design.** Both players get the same questions, seeded fairly — the faster, sharper mind wins.
- **A ladder worth climbing.** Leagues and weekly promotions give practice a point.

## Who's behind it

mathe is built by **SarkariBuddy**, a team obsessed with making learning feel like play. We're a small group of engineers, designers and educators who think the best way to get good at something is to enjoy doing it.
