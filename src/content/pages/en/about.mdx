**mathe** started with a simple belief: thinking clearly is a skill you can train, and the
best way to train it is a few honest minutes a day rather than an hour you'll never find.

## What mathe is

A growing collection of **mind games** — handcrafted logic puzzles, each with its own
ladder to climb. Today that's **Zip**, a timed path puzzle, and **Cat & Seek**, a logic
grid. More are on the way, and every one arrives in the same streak, XP and ladder system
you already know.

## Why levels, not endless random puzzles

A generator can produce infinite puzzles, and almost all of them are forgettable. Every
mathe level is authored, then machine-verified to have exactly **one** solution and graded
for difficulty. That means no dead ends, no lucky guesses, and a curve that actually ramps
— you can feel the step up rather than hoping the randomiser is kind.

## Why a daily streak

Practising alone is easy to skip. A streak gives a small, honest stake: finish one level
today and it continues, miss a day and it resets. There are no freezes and no catching up,
which is exactly what makes keeping one worth something.

## What makes mathe different

- **Every level is solvable.** Verified before it ships — never "probably fine".
- **Hints that reason.** A hint is computed from the board in front of you and advances you one step, so you still solve it yourself.
- **Free, and no account needed.** One tap to play as a guest. Sign in only if you want your progress to follow you.
- **Yours to leave.** Delete your account and data whenever you like — [here's how](/en/delete-account/).

## Who's behind it

mathe is built by a very small independent team who think the best way to get good at
something is to enjoy doing it.
