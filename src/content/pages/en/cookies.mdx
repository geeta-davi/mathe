This policy explains how the **mathe.games** website uses cookies and similar technologies. The mathe mobile app uses device storage rather than browser cookies.

## What cookies are

Cookies are small files a website stores on your device. They let the site remember choices (like your language) and understand how the site is used.

## How we use them

- **Essential** — remember your language preference and keep the site working.
- **Analytics** — measure anonymous, aggregated traffic so we can improve the site. We only set these with your consent where required.

We do not use cookies for advertising or cross-site tracking.

## Managing cookies

You can clear or block cookies in your browser settings. Blocking essential cookies may affect how the site works. Where required, we ask for your consent before setting non-essential cookies and you can change your choice at any time.

## Changes

We may update this policy and will post the new version here with an updated date.
