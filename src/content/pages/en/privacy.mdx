**mathe** is operated by SarkariBuddy ("we", "us"). This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. It applies to the mathe apps and the mathe.games website.

> This document is a template and not legal advice. Have it reviewed by a qualified professional before launch.

## Information we collect

- **Account data** — a username, and optionally an email address if you create an account.
- **Gameplay data** — the tricks you learn, duels you play, ratings, streaks and progress, so we can run matchmaking and show your stats.
- **Device & usage data** — app version, device type, language and anonymous analytics events that help us fix bugs and improve the game.

## How we use your data

- To run core features: matchmaking, leagues, leaderboards, streaks and rewards.
- To keep the game fair and secure, and to prevent cheating and abuse.
- To understand what to improve, using aggregated, non-identifying analytics.

## Sharing

We do not sell your personal data. We share limited data only with service providers who help us operate the game (for example, cloud hosting and analytics), under contracts that require them to protect it.

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data. You can request this any time by emailing **privacy@mathe.games**. You can also delete your account from within the app.

## Children

mathe is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age required in their country without appropriate consent.

## Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account is active, then delete or anonymize it within a reasonable period, unless we must keep it to meet a legal obligation.

## Changes

We may update this policy. We'll post the new version here and update the date above. Material changes will be highlighted in the app.

## Contact

Questions about privacy? Email **privacy@mathe.games**.
