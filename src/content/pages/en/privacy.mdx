**mathe** ("we", "us") operates the mathe mobile app (Google Play package `games.mathe`)
and the mathe.games website. This policy explains what we collect, why, who we share it
with, and the choices you have.

## Information we collect

- **Account and sign-in data** — if you sign in with Google, we receive your email address, name, profile picture URL and Google account ID. If you play as a guest, we create an account keyed to a device identifier instead, with no email or name.
- **Profile** — the username you choose and any avatar image you upload.
- **Gameplay data** — daily streak and activity counts, XP, which levels you have completed, and your times and scores for the leaderboards.
- **Notifications** — a push notification token for your device, and your notification preferences.
- **Product analytics** — screens you open and actions you take in the app, including **session recordings** of your in-app activity, used to find where the app is confusing or broken. Text inputs and images are masked in these recordings.
- **Crash and diagnostic data** — stack traces, device model, OS version and app version when something goes wrong.
- **Advertising identifier** — the Android advertising ID, used to serve ads and measure their performance.
- **Device and usage data** — app version, device type, language and approximate region.

## How we use your data

- To run the app: your account, level progress, streaks, XP and leaderboards.
- To send the notifications you have enabled, such as streak reminders.
- To keep results fair — for example, rejecting implausibly fast level completions.
- To find and fix crashes and usability problems.
- To show ads, which is how the app stays free.
- To understand what to improve, using aggregated analytics.

## Who we share it with

**We do not sell your personal data.** We share limited data with service providers who
help us operate the app, each under contract to protect it:

| Provider | Purpose | Data |
|---|---|---|
| Google Play & Firebase | Distribution, sign-in, analytics | Account identifiers, usage, device |
| Google AdMob | Advertising | Advertising ID, device, ad interactions |
| PostHog (EU) | Product analytics and session replay | Usage events, device, region |
| Sentry (EU) | Crash reporting | Stack traces, device, app version |
| Expo | Push notification delivery | Push token |
| Amazon Web Services, Cloudflare | Hosting, database, image storage | All of the above, at rest |

## Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete
your data.

- **Notifications** — turn any category off in the app's settings, or at the OS level.
- **Ads** — reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings to limit ad personalisation.
- **Deletion** — see [Delete your account and data](/en/delete-account/), or email us at the address below.

To exercise any of these, email **geetaapppublications@gmail.com**.

## Children

mathe is intended for a general audience and is **not directed at children under 13**. We
do not knowingly collect personal data from children below the age of consent in their
country. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, email us and we will
delete it.

## Data retention

We keep your data for as long as your account exists. When you request deletion we process
it within 30 days. Some records may be kept longer where the law requires it, or to prevent
fraud and abuse; those are deleted once no longer needed. Aggregated statistics that cannot
identify you may be retained.

## International transfers

Our servers are in the United States. Analytics and crash reporting are hosted in the
European Union. Where data moves between regions, we rely on appropriate safeguards for
that transfer.

## Changes

We may update this policy. The new version will be posted here with an updated date above,
and material changes will be highlighted in the app.

## Contact
