**mathe** (Google Play package `games.mathe`, published by geetadavis) lets you request
deletion of your account and its associated data at any time. This page explains what is
deleted, how to ask for it, and how long it takes.

## What we delete on request

- **Account and sign-in identifiers** — your email address, name, profile picture URL and Google account ID, or the device identifier if you played as a guest.
- **Profile** — your chosen username and any avatar image you uploaded.
- **Gameplay data** — daily streak and activity counts, XP, level progress and leaderboard scores.
- **Notifications** — your push notification token and notification preferences.
- **Analytics and diagnostics** — the product-analytics and crash-report records tied to your account identifier.

## How to request deletion

Email [geetaapppublications@gmail.com](mailto:geetaapppublications@gmail.com?subject=Delete%20my%20mathe%20account)
from the email address linked to your account, using the subject line
**“Delete my mathe account”**.

If you played as a guest and have no email linked, include your in-app username so we can
locate the account. We may ask you to confirm ownership before we proceed.

## Timing and retention

Requests are processed within **30 days** of verification.

Some records may be kept for longer where we are required to by law, or to prevent fraud
and abuse. Those are deleted once they are no longer needed. Aggregated statistics that
cannot identify you may be retained.
